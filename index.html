About the System

The Need for Digital Transformation

This system is the Institute's commitment to modernization. We replaced outdated, slow reporting methods (manual registers, lost emails) with a centralized, digital platform. Our mission is to ensure **maximum infrastructure uptime** and a high-quality living and learning environment for everyone.

**Key Service Areas:** We handle all non-academic infrastructure faults across **all Academic Blocks, Hostels, Faculty Quarters, and Common Areas** like grounds and cafeterias.

Core Technology and Benefits

  • Instant Automated Routing: Zero latency between reporting and assignment. The ticket is on the staff member's device in seconds.
  • Data-Driven Maintenance: We use aggregated data to predict high-risk areas and implement preventative solutions, saving long-term costs.
  • Security and Accountability: Every action is logged, providing a clear audit trail from submission to resolution, ensuring accountability.
